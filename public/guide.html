Filtering and Sorting

Filters across the top of the table:

  • All — every active token
  • Watchlist — only your starred tokens
  • Curated — tokens launched through MetaDAO's curated process
  • Permissionless — tokens launched permissionlessly
  • Migration — tokens migrated from other platforms

Click any column header to sort ascending or descending.

Sidebar

The left sidebar shows a compact token list grouped by launchpad type, with live sparkline charts and price changes. Use it to jump between tokens without returning to the landing page.

Token Detail Page

Selecting a token opens the detail view with a chart on the left and stats panels on the right.

Chart

The chart is built with TradingView Lightweight Charts and shows price data with an optional NAV overlay.

Timeframe: 24h.

Layer toggles:

  • P (Price) — smooth price line
  • C (Candles) — OHLC candlestick bars. Green for up, red for down.
  • N (NAV) — NAV overlay line with historical NAV data

Price and Candles are mutually exclusive — enabling one disables the other. NAV can be combined with either. When both price/candles and NAV are active, the area between them is filled green (above NAV) or red (below NAV).

Additional controls:

  • LOG — toggle logarithmic price scale
  • AUTO — auto-fit the chart to visible data

Crosshair: Hovering over the chart shows a metrics panel with the price, NAV, vs-NAV percentage, treasury, market cap, and other stats at that point in time.

Supply Breakdown

A visual equation showing how effective supply is derived from total supply:

Total Supply

  - DAO Treasury
  - Investor Locked (vesting)
  - Ambassador Locked (vesting)

Only DAO/MetaDAO-controlled positions are deducted — tokens supplied by independent third-party LPs are not deducted and remain in effective supply. Unclaimed fees also remain in effective supply until claimed.

Vesting deductions decrease over time as tokens unlock. For the full rationale behind each deduction, see Methodology.

Watchlist

Add tokens to your watchlist by clicking the star icon in the table or on a token's detail page.

  • Watchlisted tokens appear in the sidebar and can be filtered in the table
  • Stored in your browser's localStorage — no account needed
  • Persists across sessions on the same browser
  • Clearing browser data or switching browsers resets it

Key Concepts

Ownership Tokens

Ownership tokens are issued against a real treasury. Holding one means holding a proportional claim on the DAO's stablecoins and other assets. This is fundamentally different from memecoins or utility tokens — there's a measurable floor value based on what the treasury actually holds.

Futarchy

Futarchy is a governance mechanism where decisions are made by markets instead of votes. MetaDAO pioneered this on Solana: proposals are settled by conditional token markets rather than traditional DAO voting. Tokens normally trade on the spot FutAMM; when a proposal is active, half of the FutAMM's liquidity moves into the proposal's pass/fail markets until the proposal settles.

Why Price Does Not Equal NAV

A token's market price almost never equals its NAV exactly. Several forces create deviation:

  • Illiquidity premium/discount: Small, thinly-traded tokens may trade at persistent discounts because buyers demand compensation for the difficulty of entering and exiting positions.
  • Growth expectations: If the market believes a DAO will generate revenue or its treasury will grow, the token may trade above NAV — pricing in future value.
  • Governance risk: If the market fears mismanagement, overspending, or treasury loss, the token may trade below NAV.
  • Market sentiment: Broader crypto market conditions, narrative cycles, and speculation all affect price independently of NAV.

NAVgator makes this gap visible. Understanding why the gap exists for a particular token is the analysis that matters.
